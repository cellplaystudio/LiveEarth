LiveEarth – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026  ·  Publisher: Cellplay Studio, Pakistan  ·  Contact: cellplaystudio@gmail.com

The short version. LiveEarth has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. We run no servers that store information about you. Your favourites, recent cameras, saved places and photos stay on your phone. The app does contact other companies' services to show live cameras, maps, weather, earthquakes and ads, and this page explains exactly which ones and what they can see.

3. What stays on your phone

Your favourites, recent cameras, saved routes and parking spots, interests, and language and theme choices are stored only on your phone. Recommendations ("for you") are calculated on the phone from what you watch and save and are never uploaded. The app turns off cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of this data (allowBackup is off), so it is not copied to your Google account by the app.

5. Other services the app contacts

To work, the app asks these services for data. Each receives your IP address and basic device information, as with any network request, plus the specific request described. Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

  • YouTube / Google (embedded player and thumbnails): plays live cameras and walking tours. See policies.google.com/privacy. YouTube may set cookies or similar identifiers inside the embedded player.
  • Camera operators' own servers: some cameras and traffic cameras stream directly from the operator.
  • OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap (including Nominatim): map tiles, place search and place names.
  • MET Norway and Open-Meteo: weather for the coordinates you choose or your location.
  • USGS and NASA (EONET, GIBS): earthquakes, storms, fires and satellite imagery.
  • NOAA Aviation Weather and wheretheiss.at: aviation weather and the position of the International Space Station.
  • Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and the World Bank: place facts, photos and country figures.
  • KartaView, Panoramax and Mapillary: community street-level photos where available.
  • jsDelivr and Re:Earth Plateau: scripts and 3D city data for the Photoreal 3D screen.
  • Google Maps app and website: "Find nearby", parking, meeting points and routes open Google Maps through a link. The place you chose is part of that link.
  • Google Play services: location and Play Billing, and Google's User Messaging Platform for the consent form.
  • Google ML Kit barcode scanner: reads QR codes on your phone. The image is not uploaded.

6. Advertising and your choices

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: native ads inside the app, and full-screen ads between some actions or when you return to the app. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and, where you agree or the law allows, to personalise them.

  • Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland and several US states) you are asked for your choice the first time you open the app. You can change your mind any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • More information about how Google uses data in ads: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • LiveEarth Premium removes all ads and the data collection that comes with them.

7. Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles your payment and keeps the order records. We receive only the confirmation that the purchase is valid and never your card or payment details. If you reinstall, Restore purchase asks Google Play to confirm it again.

8. Notifications and background location

Earthquake alerts (Premium) are optional. The app checks a public earthquake feed and shows a notification; nothing about you is sent. Route tracking is started only by you. While it runs, a notification stays visible and your location is saved on your phone. It stops when you stop tracking. Alerts are informational only and are not an emergency warning service.

9. GPS photos and QR scanning

The GPS camera stamps a photo with your coordinates, address, time and optionally a compass direction, and saves it in your phone's gallery. The photo and its location stamp stay on your phone unless you share the picture yourself. Please take care when sharing: a stamped photo shows where you were. Do not photograph people in a way that breaks their privacy or the law.

10. Children

LiveEarth is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age) and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, email us and we will help remove it where we can.

11. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to withdraw consent, to object, to data portability, to complain to your data protection authority, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising apart from the AdMob use described above, which you can control as explained in section 6.

Delete your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my data removes your favourites, recents, saved tracks and parking spots and recommendations.
  • Everything: uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. Photos in your gallery are yours and stay until you delete them.
  • Data held by Google (ads, purchases): use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com.
  • Anything else: email cellplaystudio@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. Because we do not keep a user account or database, we usually hold nothing about you, and we will tell you so.

12. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR applies we rely on: consent for personalised ads and for location, camera and notification permissions (you can withdraw it at any time); contract to provide the features you ask for and to deliver Premium; and legitimate interests to keep the app secure and working and to show non-personalised ads.

13. International transfers

The services listed in section 5 operate worldwide, so your requests may be processed in countries other than yours (including the United States and countries in Europe). Those companies use their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. We are based in Pakistan but do not store your data there or anywhere else.

14. Security and retention

All connections use HTTPS (cleartext traffic is switched off). Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run a user database, so we have nothing to keep, back up or lose on our side. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

15. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change we will update this page and the date at the top, and where the change is important we will tell you in the app.
